Privacy Policy

LeaseProof is designed from the ground up to store and process all inspection records 100% offline on your iPhone.

1. Data Not Collected

LeaseProof requires no user account, no login, and no cloud server. Property addresses, checklist items, inspection notes, utility meter readings, photos, signatures, and generated PDF reports are stored exclusively in your device's local SwiftData database and private app sandbox container. We do not upload, transmit, sell, or share any user documents or personal data.

2. On-Device Processing & EXIF Stripping

All utility meter optical character recognition (Apple Vision), SHA-256 tamper hashing (CryptoKit), signature rendering (PencilKit), and PDF generation execute strictly on your device. Saved evidence photos are re-encoded locally to remove embedded EXIF and GPS metadata.

3. Camera & Photo Library Permissions

Camera access is used solely to photograph room conditions, defects, keys, and utility meter dials for inclusion in your local inspection reports. Photo Library access is used solely when you select existing photos to attach to an inspection report.

4. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Subscriptions and one-time lifetime unlocks are processed by Apple App Store and validated via RevenueCat using an anonymous, non-personally-identifiable app user identifier solely for Pro entitlement status. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, or advertising identifiers (IDFA), and we do not track you across apps or websites.
