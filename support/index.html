Frequently Asked Questions

Does LeaseProof work offline in basements or empty apartments?
Yes. 100% of checklists, camera/photo storage, Vision meter OCR, Move-In vs. Move-Out comparison, handwritten signatures, SHA-256 sealing, and PDF generation run locally with zero internet connection.

How do Move-In vs. Move-Out comparisons work?
Open any property containing a Move-In report and tap Start Move-Out Comparison. LeaseProof creates an independent snapshot of all rooms, items, baseline photos, meters, and keys so you can inspect side-by-side differences and flag new damage.

How do I restore purchases or cancel a subscription?
Tap Restore Purchases inside Settings or the upgrade screen. To manage or cancel an active subscription, open iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.
